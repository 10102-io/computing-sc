## Save on Autopilot

What you authorize. If you turn on Save on autopilot, you add a limited signing permission to your email wallet (the "Autopilot Permission") and authorize 10102 to use it for one purpose only: to create USDC timelocks through the 10102 TimeLockRouter, owned by that same wallet, that open on the date you chose at setup (the "Lock Date"). You sign the approval that lets the TimeLockRouter take USDC from your wallet yourself, at setup. Turnkey's secure hardware enforces the Autopilot Permission's limits: it cannot transfer assets to 10102 or anyone else, create gifts, approve tokens, export keys or add to its own permissions. It can only create such timelocks, and remove itself.

Lock dates. We set every lock to open on or shortly before the Lock Date. The technical limits bound the date as well: even if the Autopilot Permission were misused, a lock could open no more than about four months after the Lock Date, and it would still belong to your wallet.

Standing instruction. We use the Autopilot Permission only under the rule shown at setup: when your wallet holds at least 20 USDC, at most once per day, we lock the wallet's USDC balance until the Lock Date, up to 10,000 USDC per lock and per 30 days, and only while more than 7 days remain before the Lock Date. We exercise no other discretion over amounts or timing. We do not promise that any lock will happen at a particular time, for example when the network is congested or fees are unusually high.

How long it lasts. Each Autopilot Permission lasts up to one year, or until the Lock Date if that is sooner. We remind you before it ends.

Locks are final until their date. Under the rules of the smart contract, each timelock can be withdrawn only with your wallet's signature, to the destination you sign for, and only on or after its unlock date. You cannot cancel or shorten a timelock, and neither can we. The smart contracts that hold timelocks can be upgraded only through the public, time-delayed process described in our Upgrade Policy (https://docs.10102.io/architecture/upgrade-policy).

Stopping. You can stop Save on autopilot at any time, from your signed-in session or from the stop link in any autopilot email. Stopping removes the Autopilot Permission from your wallet; from your signed-in session you can also withdraw the USDC approval. Stopping does not affect timelocks already created. We may pause or end Save on autopilot at any time, including for legal, sanctions, security or cost reasons. We will try to tell you in advance unless the law or the circumstances prevent it.

Changes to these Terms. If we change these Terms in a way that affects Save on autopilot, we will pause autopilot for your wallet until you accept the new Terms.

Who can use it. Save on autopilot is available only to residents of the US states we list as supported at setup, using it from the United States. You confirm that you are such a resident, and you agree to turn autopilot off if you move elsewhere.
